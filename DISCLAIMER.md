# Important safety information

LibreBeacon is experimental, independent, unvalidated software. It is **not a medical device or a dependable medical or emergency alert service**. Nothing in this repository proves regulatory approval, clinical safety or reliable message delivery.

It may stop running when Windows sleeps, close without warning, lose Wi-Fi or internet access, read outdated data, receive invalid data, fail to play audio, fail to flash a light, fail to send contact notifications or send a message which nobody receives or reads. Meshtastic depends on compatible radios, network reachability and local operating conditions. Unofficial LibreLinkUp interfaces may change.

**Continue using the approved Libre application and its official alarms.** Never use LibreBeacon as the sole way to detect low or high glucose, decide treatment, summon help or monitor somebody who cannot safely manage without a reliable caregiver. Thresholds must come from the person and their clinical team. The developer has not selected clinically appropriate alarm values.

A successful “Test” or Home Assistant API request is **not proof of recipient acknowledgement**. An automated notification is not a substitute for an agreed emergency plan. If urgent assistance is needed in the UK, use 999 or 112 as appropriate.
