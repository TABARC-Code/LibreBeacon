# Technical references

Checked 17 October 2026. These references explain the third-party interfaces; they do not validate LibreBeacon as a medical or emergency system.

- [Home Assistant REST API](https://developers.home-assistant.io/docs/api/rest/) — states and service calls.
- [Home Assistant `script.turn_on`](https://www.home-assistant.io/actions/script.turn_on/) — named scripts with variables.
- [Home Assistant `notify.send_message`](https://www.home-assistant.io/actions/notify.send_message/) — targeted notification entities.
- [Meshtastic Home Assistant integration](https://github.com/meshtastic/home-assistant) — gateways and targeted `notify.mesh_*` entities.
- [Meshtastic network security](https://meshtastic.org/docs/overview/encryption/) — channel and direct-message encryption.
- [rumpelbaer/ha-librelinkup](https://github.com/rumpelbaer/ha-librelinkup) — community integration providing glucose readings and related entities.
- [Microsoft Phone Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/develop/windows-integration/phone-link-notification-integration) — platform integration; not a general notification-scraping interface.
